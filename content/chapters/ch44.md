# XLIV. The Kept Gate

*―  to be written  ―*

*DESIGN NOTE (locked): The road home runs through a cellar in Nesmé, where a mirror laid back together on swept stone has been counting under the quiet, and an orc has been sitting beside a shield that says KEEP, waiting in a way that is visible from across the room. The knife is handed back; a word is carved; and a sleeper behind glass is owed an asking, and a name.*
