*THE MECHANISM (locked): Chapter XLV writes the Vault of Glass up to the moment of decision — release her, or end her — and STOPS. This Epilogue is a letter. It addresses each of the seven by name and asks them to write back: what does your character do, and why? Blank pages follow for the answers. When the answers come, the true final chapter will be written, and sent. One last session, played by mail. The title earns itself twice over — anamnesis is what happened to the characters, and then it is what this book asks of its readers.*
